How to measure

Use a non-stretch tape, snug against the body without compressing it. Measure the neck, abdomen, and waist on bare skin. Keep your shoulders relaxed and look straight ahead.

Neck
Just below the larynx (Adam's apple), perpendicular to the long axis of the neck. Keep the tape as close to horizontal as anatomically possible, at the same height in front and back, avoiding the shoulder muscles.
Abdomen (navel)
Across the belly button with arms at your sides, at the end of a normal, relaxed exhale. Keep the tape level.
Waist (narrowest point)
At the smallest circumference, usually between the navel and the bottom of the breastbone. Keep the tape level, arms at your sides, and measure at the end of a normal exhale.
Hips
Around the widest part of the buttocks, with feet together and the tape level. The Navy guide measures over its physical-training shorts; avoid shaping or compressive clothing.

For this app, take up to three readings per site. We keep the raw readings and use their mean without half-inch rounding. Try to measure at the same time of day, such as in the morning before eating.

Site guidance: 2021 Navy body composition guide, section 3. This app uses the traditional Hodgdon–Beckett equation with its own repeated-reading approach.
